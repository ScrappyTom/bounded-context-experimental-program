## Lifecycle

```text
common exact acquisition
        ↓
authentic pending-result pressure
        ↓
common first-fit relief
        ↓
        ├─ A0: exact matrix + incremental decision
        └─ A1: batched fallible scaffold + same exact work
        ↓
actor-selected construction milestone
        ↓
fresh current verification frame
        ↓
A1 scaffold removed; exact artifact retained in both
        ↓
check → repair → effect uptake → recheck → closure disposition
```

The scaffold is phase-conditional. Solace and Orchard suggest semantic residue
can help initiate construction yet consume or distort verification. The exact
artifact is durable work state; the scaffold is temporary capital whose value
must pay for production, residency, admission failures, and induced demand.

## Maintenance law

The common prefix contains no maintenance. After the fork, A1 queues only
newly externalized, previously visible exact source results. A bounded call
occurs after two queued result objects or at a frozen construction flush and
proposes at most eight anchored claims.

```text
valid anchor + provenance + requirement link → merge claim
invalid claim                              → charge and discard
zero valid or over-budget update           → preserve prior scaffold
```

No repair or retry is allowed. The actor continues after every maintenance
outcome. Transport yield and semantic quality are measured inside the
trajectory rather than used as an all-or-nothing gate.
