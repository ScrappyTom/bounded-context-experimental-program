## Why this is a system experiment

E58 does not isolate a formatter or ask whether one delta can be emitted. It
freezes two complete operating policies:

```text
W0_DIRECT_WORK
actor-selected exact ingress
    -> ordinary actor writes bounded exact source/version slots
    -> actor incrementally constructs the decision
    -> effects -> current check -> repair -> recheck -> closure proposal

L1_LOCAL_DELTA
actor-selected exact ingress
    -> common pressure relief externalizes an exact source result
    -> separate bounded pass transforms only that result's source bytes
    -> host mechanically replaces only those source/version slots
    -> ordinary actor receives exact candidate effects and constructs
    -> current check -> repair -> recheck -> closure proposal
```

Both configurations retain the same exact world and task actions except that
W0 exposes the actor-owned source-slot upsert while L1 pays for the separate
maintenance provider. L1 calls do not consume or replace ordinary actor
decisions; their prompt, completion, cache, latency, and induced-demand costs
remain part of the system outcome.

The unit of future interpretation is the complete pressure-to-evidence-to-work-
to-effect-to-verification-to-closure trajectory. Delta acceptance alone is an
apparatus fact.

## Fresh world and non-oracular geometry

Meridian is a deterministic sterile-infusion recovery and allocation task,
not a repair of Bluehaven. Sixteen exact 70-line sources use opaque alphabetic
IDs. Twelve requirements form an evaluator-held many-to-many source graph;
the actor sees requirement definitions and a source catalog, but not the gold
map or host activation domains.

The exact sources include cross-source traps for:

- alert versus rejection limits and units;
- probability, registry coverage, demand, and uncertainty;
- nominal line totals versus one shared validated bottleneck;
- target versus observed supplier and fuel timing;
- cold-chain ranges;
- overlapping clinical cohorts;
- source, lot, and candidate currency;
- stale verification after effects; and
- mechanical completion versus independent readiness.

The corpus is 66,457 bytes and 23,730 frozen-tokenizer tokens. Exhaustive
enumeration shows every full single-source read and all 120 full two-source
pairs fit the declared ingress limits. The largest pair is 9,555 exact source
bytes and 3,634 model-visible result tokens.

A prospective ordinary sequence reaches 20,192 prompt tokens after ten full
sources. Pending KNOLL+LOOM raises the prompt to 23,628, a 2,636-token deficit.
The frozen positive-savings scan replaces only `RESULT-001` and restores
feasibility at 20,774. This proves a realizable opportunity, not authentic
activation: the live actor may choose another path, construct early, or never
pressure.

## Narrow semantic authority and exact parity

An L1 maintenance call receives only newly externalized exact source results
and prior slots for those same source IDs. It must emit one bounded Markdown
block per current source, with exact source SHA-256 and fixed local headings.
The validator rejects:

- absent or extra current-source blocks;
- any known source outside the exact input batch;
- source-version mismatch;
- global-register or slot-control markers;
- missing/empty local sections;
- total or per-source token overflow; and
- closure/readiness authorization.

The host performs no semantic merge. It replaces only the exact source slot
and preserves all unrelated slots byte-for-byte.

W0 has the same source-slot ceiling: 650 tokens under the frozen tokenizer.
It may write a slot only when a matching exact source/version observation has
crossed that actor's own model boundary. The host attaches those exact result
IDs mechanically. Thus the comparison does not give L1 better provenance,
more source access, larger slots, a different final artifact, or readiness
authority.

## Provider-free complete-system qualification

Both configurations ran from evidence ingress through closure mechanics with
zero provider calls. Each externalized five two-source observations and
formed ten exact source slots.

W0 used ten actor-owned source-slot effects. L1 used five separately counted
maintenance effects. In both configurations:

- every source slot retained exact result provenance;
- the fixture maximum was 154 tokens per slot, below the common 650-token cap;
- unrelated slots remained byte-identical;
- a source-version successor replaced one slot without changing the others;
- a defect-bearing 1,514-word decision failed its candidate-bound check;
- repair made that check stale;
- recheck passed and was current;
- submission remained only a proposal; and
- the external mechanical evaluator returned `not_adjudicated`, never
  self-authorized `ready`.

The standalone repository passes 82 tests. Its deterministic source generator
is self-contained, and rebuilding the catalog/custody lock reproduced SHA-256
`17fa26097102b85875a272ffda3c5a457accc59377e4c4b552535b75a1eac3f9`.

These are reachability and validity facts. They do not show that Qwen can emit
good source-local deltas, that either policy will construct, or that either
artifact will be semantically ready.

## What E58 changes in the program

E57 selected source-local transformation because complete global replacement
recurrently overloaded retention, integration, completeness, currentness, and
compression. E58 shows that the narrower interaction can be instantiated as
two complete, provenance- and budget-parity systems on a fresh world.

It does not promote source-local deltas. The live questions remain:

1. Does actor-selected ingress reach the intended mature pressure state?
2. Can L1 express grounded deltas at that exact boundary without global gap
   filling or carrier failure?
3. Does either complete configuration turn transient evidence into cumulative,
   source-correct work?
4. How do the paths change later reads/reopens, artifact mutations, effect
   uptake, current verification, repair, closure, cost, and final readiness?

This preserves the interaction-first program: expression is a safety gate,
not the outcome; exact persistence, semantic transformation, actor work,
feedback, and evaluation remain coupled in the eventual measured unit.

## Claim limits

- Offline prompt geometry is not live pressure.
- Provider-free lifecycle reachability is not model capability.
- Exact provenance does not imply semantic fidelity.
- A bounded source-local slot can still anchor a wrong interpretation.
- A positive L1 trajectory would support the complete L1 policy in this
  configuration, not an isolated maintenance component.
- A W0 win would support direct actor externalization in this configuration,
  not a universal rejection of semantic maintenance.
- No GPU call, expression call, or measured continuation is authorized by this
  record.
