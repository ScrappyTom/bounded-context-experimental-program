## Interpretation

The E61 admission rule was structurally mismatched to Meridian's task world.
Every source-local semantic object that preserved at least one named governing
relationship could encounter the same lexical rejection.

This sharpens the design distinction:

```text
owner source
    licenses a claim and owns the mutable slot

relationship object
    may be named by that licensed claim

attributed source
    would assert custody or content from another exact object
```

Only the third case is equivalent to unsupported absent-source writing. A
future mechanical interface can potentially validate owner and mutation scope
strictly while separately allowing relationship-object identities that occur
in the exact owner bytes.
