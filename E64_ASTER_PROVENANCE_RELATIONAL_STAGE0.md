## Provenance-local relational contract

Every admitted L1 claim separates:

1. mutable owner source and exact version;
2. one delivered exact result;
3. one exact, unique, complete evidence-line quote;
4. host-resolved line number and host-derived span hash;
5. source-reported fact versus source-reported relationship;
6. named referents present in both the statement and owner evidence;
7. non-authoritative source attribution; and
8. host-derived current/stale status.

Referents may be named without gaining mutation authority. The carrier cannot
create absent-source slots or emit derived cross-source claims. Genuine joint
claims belong to the exact task artifact and require complete citations. Full
historical deltas remain in external custody; only the bounded current register
is actor-visible.

The host validates custody, scope, span existence, version, and currentness. It
does not validate predicates, modality, units, probability meaning, authority,
qualifications, or truth. Frozen semantic review and final artifact evaluation
remain mandatory.
