## Result

The post-construction semantic register did not earn verification-phase value
in this local lifecycle comparison.

Both arms began from the exact same strong-partial Solace candidate, evidence
ledger, latest undelivered effect, source catalog, candidate-bound checker,
bounded patch surface, model, seed, and budgets. A1 alone retained the frozen
twenty-claim construction register.

Both actors immediately checked the candidate, read BASTION/CIPHER and then
DELTA/INDIGO, and repaired all four frozen substantive concept groups. Their
paths then diverged:

| Measure | A0 exact artifact only | A1 artifact + register |
|---|---:|---:|
| Actor/provider calls | 10 | 4 |
| Serialized tokens | 169,577 | 74,060 |
| Candidate mutations | 4 | 1 |
| Current checks | 4 | 1 |
| Initial prompt | 8,693 | 15,339 |
| Final effect entered a later actor decision | no | no |
| Submitted | no | no |

A0 used an iterative check–patch loop. It reached a mechanically all-substantive-
gates-pass candidate after three repair/check cycles, then misunderstood the
remaining heading-order failure as capitalization and changed all eleven
headings without converting them to the frozen exact eight-heading structure.
Its final effect could not fit into another decision. The next packet was
21,990 prompt tokens with no eligible exact body able to restore feasibility.

A1 used the register plus exact DELTA/INDIGO and receipt-backed BASTION/CIPHER
to make one broad four-section patch. That is evidence that the frozen semantic
state retained repair salience after its raw owner sources left residency. But
the fixed register consumed 6,646 initial prompt tokens. Even after both source
results were externalized, the pending patch effect left the next packet at
21,145 tokens. A1 never saw its own repair effect and never rechecked.

## Systems interpretation

E69 and E72 together support a phase-conditional—not representation-global—
hypothesis:

```text
acquisition and early construction
    provenance-bound semantic residue can scaffold conversion
    of transient exact evidence into task work

construction
    exact incremental artifact increasingly carries cumulative meaning

verification
    the same fixed semantic stock can become redundant or harmful
    relative to exact artifact + current check + targeted exact evidence
```

The local A0>A1 lifecycle depth does not establish that exact artifact alone is
a complete verification architecture. A0 also failed. Its append-only sequence
of check observations, candidate effects, actions, and receipts became
unrelievable even though the useful current verification state was small.

The next failure is therefore an interaction among:

```text
scaffold retention/demotion
× exact artifact state
× candidate/effect/check currentness
× actionable validator feedback
× verification-history lifecycle
× bounded patch transport
```

This is not a reason to tune the Solace register or rerun the same donor.

## Apparatus lesson

The provider-free E71 repair fixture reached a mechanical pass but itself used
the unsupported `48 samples per round` relation. That exposes an important
qualification error:

```text
provider-free lifecycle reachability
does not imply
semantic correctness of the fixture or evaluator
```

Future checks must expose task-derived expected versus observed structures and
must test material relations—not merely co-occurrence of required strings.
They remain common external feedback, not readiness authority.
