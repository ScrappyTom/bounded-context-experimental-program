## Interaction semantics

The treatment changes a feedback relation. In D0, model-authored integration
can influence the actor but remains outside the exact deliverable. In A1, the
same class of accepted bytes becomes task-world state, changes candidate
identity, enters later checks, and can be revised by ordinary actor actions.
Subsequent demand, maintenance inputs, pressure, cost, artifact revisions, and
closure may therefore diverge endogenously.

Maintenance receives only the authoritative task, its prior accepted bounded
state, and the exact result just externalized. Source IDs are mechanically
limited to the prior's grounded sources plus the new exact observation. An A1
actor revision can feed back into later maintenance only for cited sources that
had already crossed that actor's boundary. No catalog-only evidence is
injected.
