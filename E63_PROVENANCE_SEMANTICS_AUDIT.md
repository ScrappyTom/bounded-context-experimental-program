evidence basis, relationship referents, assertion attribution, multi-source
support, and version currentness. Ten exact historical fixtures from Meridian,
E61, Bluehaven, and Cedar reproduced their declared dispositions.

- Bluehaven's unseen S07 semantic completion from S01–S06 inputs.

semantic failures: the reversed Meridian sample-staleness predicate and
Cedar's 5.8-hour/42-percent conversion into km/h and humidity. Thus the
contract protects custody and mutation scope without pretending to validate
semantic truth.

## What changed

E61 motivated a three-way distinction between source owner, relationship
object, and absent-source attribution. E63 sharpens that into five operational
properties:

1. record kind and mutable namespace;
2. exact source/version evidence basis;
3. named relationship referents;
4. assertion mode and attribution; and
5. complete support set plus currentness.

Pure per-source slots remain appropriate only for source-reported facts and
relationships. Genuine cross-source synthesis requires task-native derived
work with every supporting exact source; it cannot be assigned arbitrarily to
one source.
