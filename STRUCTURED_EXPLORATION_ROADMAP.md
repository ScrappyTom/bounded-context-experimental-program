## Research-phase reset

The program is not ready to optimize a nearly known context architecture. It
has qualified several trustworthy execution primitives and ruled out several
local formulations, but it has not established how a bounded actor reliably
turns a large information world into useful action.

The active objective is therefore:

> Map the capability terrain of large-world bounded work before selecting or
> optimizing a resident-state architecture.

After E37, “map” must not mean routing each label below into an isolated
component study. The next governing contract is
`SYSTEM_INTERACTION_EXPLORATION.md`: compare viable whole configurations and
observe how their information functions change one another over time.

This is a change in research phase, not a weakening of evidence standards and
not a rejection of prior work. Frozen studies, negative results, exact custody,
and current branches remain unchanged. The change is that new work will sample
materially different task situations and failure functions before refining a
single mechanism.

The program will be exact about what happened and provisional about why it
happened, what conceptual role a representation played, and which mechanism
should follow.

## Minimal validity envelope

Discovery probes remain inside a small fixed envelope. Preserve:

1. exact task and starting-world custody;
2. exact model, runtime, template, sampler, and tool identity;
3. the complete model-visible transcript, including whether each result or
   effect actually crossed a model boundary;
4. one attempt per declared decision and no hidden retry or outcome-dependent
   replacement;
5. hard context, response, call, token, time, and authorization limits;
6. exact candidate/world/effect identity and deterministic admission;
7. independent artifact and readiness evaluation when work quality or closure
   is claimed; and
8. no post-outcome alteration of a measured cell.

Add more apparatus only when it is necessary to observe the question
truthfully. A schema, role, state object, router, checkpoint, validator, or
terminal workflow is not required merely because it would make a proposed
architecture cleaner.

Exploratory cells may use lighter packaging and fewer replications than a
confirmatory study. They may not weaken raw custody, causal accounting, or
outcome evaluation.

## Discovery and confirmation are different stages

### Discovery

Discovery uses small, low-commitment probes across materially different
authentic tasks and work modes. Its purpose is to locate capability boundaries,
generate competing explanations, and identify configurations in which a
function matters.

A discovery result may show behavioral activity or a new failure boundary. It
does not promote a mechanism. One or two calls can answer a narrow discovery
question when the claim is correspondingly narrow. Long rollouts are justified
only when the phenomenon itself is recurrence, amortization, repair, or
lifecycle behavior.

After a valid negative, move laterally unless an apparatus defect made the
intended question unevaluable, dominated or obscured the target behavior,
broke arm parity, made the required control surface unreachable, or turned a
host-provided view into an oracle. Preserve the failed attempt and treat any
repaired probe as a new cell. Do not repair a prompt, schema, packet, or role
repeatedly just to make one formulation win.

### Confirmation

A costly confirmation bank, repeated mechanism optimization, or architecture
promotion becomes eligible only when substantially the same practically
important behavior or failure has appeared in at least two independent task
worlds. A cheap controlled contrast may be used earlier as discovery when it is
the simplest way to learn whether an arrangement is behaviorally active; its
claim remains local.

An independent world requires a distinct task instance, starting world, and
source/candidate lineage with no shared trajectory or donor boundary. A new
seed, serialization, artifact presentation, or work mode over the same task and
lineage may establish local stability but does not satisfy this recurrence gate.

Confirmation requires:

- at least two plausible explanations or interventions;
- a treatment that does not encode the desired answer;
- independently assessable downstream quality;
- a prospectively frozen feedback horizon; and
- a reason the comparison could change an architectural decision.

The recurrence gate has one narrow safety exception. One severe authorization
breach, irreversible effect, false closure, or custody/integrity failure may
justify a single bounded forensic or safety-confirmation study before
independent recurrence. The exception exists to characterize and contain
high-cost failure; it cannot promote an architecture, authorize an optimization
bank, or waive owner review.

## Broad endpoint coding

For every exploratory trajectory, reconstruct:

1. what exact information was accessible;
2. what the actor actually observed;
3. what remained literal at each decision;
4. what semantic relationship or qualification the task appeared to require;
5. what substantive action or work product was formed;
6. which admitted effects became model-visible;
7. what resource became terminal; and
8. where continuation stopped.

Code the endpoint broadly:

- relevant information not discovered;
- candidate information discovered but poorly selected or accessed;
- dispersed information observed but not assembled;
- governing relation represented but not bound into action;
- information assembled but acquisition continued;
- intended work formed but not externalized;
- action organization selected but no legal expression reached the environment;
- action attempted with missing relationships or qualifications;
- action chosen but not expressed through the interface;
- effect occurred but current-world continuity failed;
- effect visible and check run, but the check did not discriminate the defect;
- feedback arrived but repair failed;
- artifact correct but testing or repair continued;
- artifact incorrect but fluent closure occurred;
- response-level continuation absent, unused, or mechanically ineligible;
- inspection/readiness/closure failed;
- resource budget ended before the capability became observable; or
- useful completion.

Multiple codes may apply in sequence. These codes describe literal boundaries;
they do not assign a latent cause to the model.

## Sampling and routing cadence

### 1. Orient

State the complete feedback loop, the co-active functions whose interaction is
unknown, why current evidence is insufficient, and the authentic state that
can expose their joint behavior.

### 2. Probe

Run every prospectively declared viable configuration through a shared
feedback horizon. Preserve complete trajectories, persistent state, effects,
feedback, artifacts, costs, and terminal conditions.

### 3. Diagnose

Classify substitution, complementarity, interference, nonactivation, apparatus
censoring, and failure migration. List multiple explanations including model
capability, information layout, task geometry, action interface, call budget,
evaluation, and procedure.

### 4. Search laterally for recurrence

Move the complete configuration contrast to an independent world that could
exhibit the same interaction. Do not make the original world incrementally
harder merely to reproduce it.

### 5. Promote only when earned

Design a controlled comparison only after recurrence and only when downstream
quality can adjudicate competing explanations.

### 6. Stop for owner review

Do not automatically implement a successor when evidence changes the
conceptual map. Stop when:

- a new broad capability boundary appears;
- a result invalidates the framing of the planned next study;
- two plausible directions require a substantive owner choice;
- a probe would cross from discovery into architecture optimization; or
- the next step would add a permanent role, schema, controller, or router.

## Checkpoint memo required before new measured work

Each checkpoint must answer:

1. What complete bounded-work loop and operating regime are being investigated?
2. Which present assumptions are architectural commitments rather than earned
   evidence?
3. Which apparatus requirements are necessary for validity?
4. Which apparatus requirements exist only because of the current proposed
   solution?
5. Which co-active information functions may substitute, complement, or
   interfere, and what materially different explanations remain plausible?
6. What is the smallest set of viable whole configurations that can expose the
   interaction through a shared downstream outcome?
7. What will not be built or optimized during this exploration wave?
8. At what point is owner review required before controlled continuation?

The memo must also state whether the proposed interaction has recurred in an
independent world. If not, the next measured work remains local interaction
discovery and its claims must be scoped accordingly.
