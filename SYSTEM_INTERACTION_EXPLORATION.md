## Decision

The next research unit is a **whole bounded-work configuration operating over
time**, not an isolated memory object, capability label, prompt field, or host
operation.

The active question is:

> Under authentic context pressure, how do evidence ingress, exact residency,
> semantic persistence, exact work-product persistence, action expression,
> effects, feedback, repair, resumption, and closure jointly determine whether
> information becomes useful cumulative work?

This corrects a drift in the first capability-terrain tranche. E37 sampled
different tasks at low occupancy and revealed expression and readiness
failures. It did not activate context pressure, semantic persistence, or
incremental work, and it contained no matched lifecycle contrast. E37 is a
useful low-pressure reference regime. It is not an answer to the intended
interaction question.

## Why component-by-component routing is insufficient

The system is a feedback loop:

```text
accessible exact world
        ↓
evidence requests and delivery
        ↓
resident exact + semantic + work-product state
        ↓
relationship formation and intended work
        ↓
legal action expression and admitted effect
        ↓
candidate-bound feedback and readiness decision
        ↓
new information demand, residency, and work state
        ↺
```

Changing any part changes the later state in which every other part is valued.
A digest can create more demand. Incremental work can reduce memory demand or
anchor a bad interpretation. A check can trigger repair or authorize false
closure. Reentry can restore orientation or reproduce an incorrect control
disposition. Context relief can preserve opportunity while changing cache cost
and future co-residency.

Consequently, a sequence of isolated questions such as “does a digest work?”,
“does partial work work?”, and “does reentry work?” cannot by itself identify a
viable system. The value of each operation is conditional on what else is
active and on the trajectory it induces.

## Non-isolation rule

A new measured study is interaction-eligible only if it satisfies all of the
following:

1. The unit of comparison is a viable end-to-end operating configuration, not
   a single component qualification result.
2. At least two information functions can be active together and influence a
   later shared outcome.
3. The study follows the feedback horizon far enough to observe induced demand,
   work-product change, effect uptake, feedback, and closure or a declared
   terminal resource.
4. Every arm retains exact external custody, ordinary actor access, reversible
   recovery, and independent artifact/readiness evaluation.
5. No arm is merely “relief versus death.” The qualified mechanical pressure
   substrate is common infrastructure for every viable post-pressure arm.
6. An apparatus gate such as action-transport qualification may precede the
   study, but it is not counted as evidence that a system configuration works.
7. A component ablation becomes appropriate only after a joint configuration
   demonstrates a meaningful trajectory-level signal. Ablation is for later
   explanation, not initial architecture selection.

The program may still report where a trajectory literally failed. It must not
turn those labels into a queue of isolated component experiments.

### Closed historical question

The first checkpoint should study the interaction between two different ways
of making cognition persistent while exact evidence streams through a bounded
context:

- **source-bound semantic residue**: a bounded, attributed, version-bound,
  non-authoritative representation of an exact source that remains reopenable;
- **incremental exact work product**: model-authored claims, sections, decision
  records, or other task-native partial work stored exactly as mutable artifact
  state.

These functions may substitute for one another, complement one another, or
interfere:

- semantic residue may preserve source meaning before an artifact is ready;
- incremental work may preserve applied relationships more economically than a
  separate note;
- semantic residue may improve later work, or merely redirect acquisition;
- partial work may reduce reopen demand, or anchor early mistakes;
- the joint configuration may outperform both, duplicate their cost, or amplify
  premature commitment.

The question is not which object is intrinsically best. It is how each complete
configuration changes the subsequent information economy.

## Persistence operations

The configured persistence operations are not passive optional tools. Earlier
workspace controls showed that an ordinary actor may never select a maintenance
action before it becomes unreachable. Stage 0 must therefore freeze mechanical
activation events and protect enough control headroom for the relevant
configuration to execute them once, with no retry.

### Source-bound semantic residue

When an exact source body becomes nonresident under the common pressure rule,
the semantic-persistence configurations invoke a prospectively fixed,
bounded source-digestion operation. Its output must be:

- source- and version-bound;
- non-authoritative and explicitly lossy;
- replaceable rather than append-only;
- free of readiness, progress, and submission instructions;
- separately token- and call-accounted; and
- paired with the exact reopen handle.

The digester's cost and any induced new source demand belong to the system
outcome. A residue is not credited merely for fidelity or behavioral salience.

If the scout begins from a historical pressure prefix, no unread source may be
digested and no prior semantic object may be invented retroactively. Stage 0
must state whether already delivered exact sources receive a charged initial
residue pass at the divergence boundary or remain receipt-only in every arm.
That choice is common, prospective, and fixed before inference.

### Incremental exact work product

The work-persistence configurations expose a task-native exact artifact surface
and a mechanically triggered work-externalization pass that allows bounded,
independently inspectable progress before the complete final artifact exists.
The trigger may be delivery of a prospectively declared evidence batch or the
imminent demotion of newly consumed exact evidence; it may not be “the host
thinks synthesis is ready.” Depending on the task, the work product may be:

- a cited evidence/decision matrix;
- an exact section or claim packet;
- a partial implementation with explicit current candidate identity; or
- another bounded artifact whose contribution to the final work can be scored.

This state is model-authored work, not host-authored semantic memory. It remains
exactly versioned and externally visible. The actor may revise or delete it.
Stage 0 must prevent the intermediate format from encoding the gold answer or a
readiness judgment.

The pass receives the exact current partial work and only evidence already
delivered to that arm. Its output replaces the prior partial-work state rather
than appending indefinitely. Production calls, invalid outputs, carrier cost,
and later actor use or nonuse are part of the configuration's measured
economics.

## Bounded resumption inside the interaction

Resumption should not be studied as a fifth isolated treatment. It should be a
common lifecycle event through which the four configurations pass.

A defensible trigger may be a prospectively task-declared mechanical event,
provided that event is defined before inference and does not assert semantic
sufficiency. It may also be the reactive condition already earned by prior
work: first-fit has exhausted every eligible body and the next legitimate
packet still cannot preserve the frozen reserves. The E39 design used
only the latter rule; it does not schedule a fresh frame merely because a
coverage label or call count is reached. If reactive reentry occurs, each arm
receives only its own legitimately accumulated persistent state:

- exact current candidate/work product;
- its receipts and/or source-bound residues according to configuration;
- current candidate-bound feedback;
- exact handles to everything else; and
- no investigator-selected unread source or readiness cue.

If an arm never reaches the condition, reentry is simply nonactivated. The
common policy and admissibility rules—not identical event timing after actor
behavior diverges—provide parity.

## Interaction outcomes

Measure the complete path, not only the next action:

- exact sources requested, delivered, demoted, and reopened;
- novel acquisition versus recovery of prior evidence;
- resident exact, semantic, and work-product tokens by decision;
- semantic-residue production and rewrite cost;
- intermediate-work creation, revision, deletion, and later use;
- first substantive construction and first candidate effect;
- whether cited relationships and qualifications survive into final work;
- effect uptake, check currency, feedback discrimination, and repair;
- artifact quality and independently adjudicated readiness;
- premature closure and correct nonclosure;
- actor, maintenance, and total calls/tokens;
- cache reuse, latency, and pressure-relief work; and
- the resource or functional boundary that becomes terminal.

Do not collapse these into a single utility score. Report the vector and the
observed feedback loops.

## Interaction interpretations

The scout should distinguish at least these outcomes:

- **substitution**: one persistence channel makes the other redundant or
  reduces its marginal value;
- **complementarity**: the joint configuration enables work or quality that
  neither channel enables alone;
- **interference**: the joint configuration increases demand, anchoring,
  inconsistency, false closure, or cost;
- **failure migration**: a configuration solves one boundary but moves the
  terminal constraint to expression, action bandwidth, feedback, or closure;
- **nonactivation**: the planned persistence or pressure operation never
  becomes live, making that cell non-diagnostic for the interaction; and
- **apparatus censoring**: transport or evaluator failure prevents the intended
  closed loop from becoming observable.

An interaction claim requires literal evidence from more than one
configuration. A single successful artifact does not establish
complementarity; a single failure does not establish interference.
