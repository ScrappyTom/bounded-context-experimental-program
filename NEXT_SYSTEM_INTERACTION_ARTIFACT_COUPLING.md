## Why this is the next system question

E40 did not leave one inactive component waiting to be repaired. It exposed a
closed-loop interaction failure:

```text
bounded evidence ingress
× host-owned pressure relief and exact reopen
× bounded model-authored semantic sidecar
× whole-final-only artifact action
× no partial verification surface
                    ↓
evidence and semantic prose accumulated temporarily,
but no exact cumulative task work or feedback cycle formed
```

The next work must therefore test a different complete information economy,
not qualify a note, patch action, or checker in isolation.

The active question is:

> Under authentic context pressure, does binding model-authored semantic
> integration to an exact, incrementally revisable, requirement- and
> source-bound task artifact—then exposing candidate-bound partial feedback—
> allow evidence to become cumulative work before replacement, residency,
> decision, or closure limits become terminal?

## Non-isolation contract

Every measured configuration must execute a complete loop containing:

1. actor-selected evidence ingress;
2. exact external custody and positive-savings pressure relief;
3. semantic integration over evidence already delivered to that arm;
4. exact, versioned task-work externalization;
5. ordinary actor uptake of that work;
6. candidate- or work-version-bound verification;
7. repair opportunity; and
8. externally adjudicated readiness and closure.

No provider call earns scientific standing merely because it can emit a note,
section, patch, or check action. Expression qualification is an apparatus gate.
The measured object is the resulting trajectory and feedback loop.

## Candidate whole-system contrast

Stage 0 qualified a small finite contrast rather than a large component matrix.
The frozen two-system design is:

| System | Semantic integration carrier | Task-work surface | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| `D0_DETACHED` | bounded replacement evidence ledger remains a non-authoritative sidecar | ledger replacement, incremental decision-section revision, or complete decision replacement | current composite-candidate check |
| `A1_COUPLED` | the same accepted ledger bytes replace the exact task-required evidence-ledger file | the same ordinary actions plus maintenance-origin candidate effects | current composite-candidate check with stale/current binding after every effect |

Both systems keep batching, ordinary reads, exact reopen, host-owned relief,

patch tool"; both actors already have the same incremental section action. It
changes where maintenance interpretation lives, how it survives context
turnover, what the actor can do with it, and what feedback becomes available.

## Measured disposition

The exact D0/A1 continuation completed 40 actor and 19 maintenance calls with
one attempt per call, zero retries, and 875,749 serialized tokens. Coupling was
strongly active: A1 changed the exact candidate before actor call 1, reached a

Both systems nevertheless exhausted twenty actor calls without submission.
Each final candidate passed fourteen of fifteen mechanical gates but missed the
1,600-word floor. Candidate-bound semantic adjudication classified both as
strong partial and not ready. The coupled candidate was broader on cost and
readiness governance but carried the already-completed E39/E40 design forward

coverage elsewhere.

The interaction therefore has local behavioral leverage but no demonstrated
useful-completion or semantic-quality advantage. A1 also changed maintenance,
reentry, cache, effect, and wall-time economics, confirming that the causal
unit is the complete feedback system rather than an isolated ledger feature.

The exact E46 fork is closed to longer continuation, prompt repair, semantic
budget tuning, and same-prefix replication. The only selected successor is an
offline fresh-world transfer design that preserves viable complete systems and
budgets a real post-construction path through effect uptake, independent check,
repair, recheck, and closure. A recurrence that again accelerates construction
without improving candidate-bound quality should deprioritize the route.

## Exact incremental work requirements

The A1 artifact must be task-native and independently inspectable. A valid
shape may be a requirement-keyed evidence/decision matrix plus bounded charter
sections, provided it obeys all of these rules:

- every claim or section has exact source-ID/provenance bindings;
- the current work object has an exact version and content hash;
- revisions replace named entries or sections instead of appending an
  unbounded note history;
- the actor can inspect, revise, delete, and assemble the work;
- admitted effects explicitly identify prior and current versions;
- checks state exactly which work/candidate version they evaluated;
- stale feedback is mechanically labeled after any subsequent effect;
- the structure contains no gold answer, readiness judgment, submission cue,
  or investigator-selected semantic relevance; and
- full prior versions remain externally custodied and exactly reopenable.

This makes the artifact a durable external cognition surface without making it
authoritative truth.

Fewer reads, earlier mutation, or faster submission is not a win unless the
artifact and readiness outcomes are correct.

## Interpretive questions

The scout should ask how the systems change one another:

- Does exact partial work reduce repeated raw recovery, or merely anchor early
  interpretations?
- Does partial feedback correct errors, or consume scarce decisions without
  changing work?
- Does embedding integration in the artifact preserve provenance better than a
  bounded sidecar, or make the artifact incoherent under repeated revision?
- Does batching become more valuable when evidence can be externalized into
  task work, or does it still accelerate turnover faster than integration?
- Does artifact currentness improve construction and verification flow, or
  move the bottleneck to final assembly and closure?

These are interaction and feedback questions. No single intermediate object is
the optimization target.
