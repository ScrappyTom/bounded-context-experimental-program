and an exact unique anchor substring from delivered evidence. The host derives
anchor bytes and offsets, exact complete containing-line context, hashes,
source/version/result binding, currentness, and reopen custody without semantic

adjacent qualification. Mechanical validation does not establish predicate
truth, completeness, or downstream usefulness.

## Admission and fallback

- Malformed or over-budget global envelope: reject all, charge the call,
  preserve prior register, continue.
- Parseable records: validate independently.
- Duplicate IDs or per-source aggregate budget failure: reject affected
  records atomically.
- Valid subset: merge into owner source/version slots.
- Zero valid records: prior register remains byte-identical.
- No repair, retry, silent truncation, inferred citation, or semantic host fix.
- Omitted claims never imply source or requirement coverage.

Exact historical outputs and rejected diagnostics remain externally
custodied. Only the bounded current register is model-facing.
