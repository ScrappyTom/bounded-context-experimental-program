# H05 close-transfer readiness discrimination — blocked plan

Date: 2026-08-22

## Question

Can a bounded model-authored progress-reconciliation package distinguish a
closure-ready state from a defect-bearing state, closing the former while
identifying or repairing a concrete deficiency in the latter?

## Why this replaces the prior framing

H05 did not close a complete artifact. Exact-hash reconciliation showed that
its unchanged submission was a known strong partial. H05 therefore supplies a
premature-closure observation, not a positive ready case.

The next defensible test needs readiness frozen independently before any
maintenance or treatment output. Submission is not inherently progress.

## Frozen design if eligible donors are later found

Use one independently closure-ready and one independently defect-bearing
authentic boundary with:

- exact reconstructed actor packets;
- stable exact evidence and candidate state;
- no pending result/effect;
- the same model/runtime, tools, reserve, and one-attempt policy;
- an authentic contemporaneous no-progress control;
- the H05 plain-text five-field maintenance interface unchanged; and
- direct candidate-bound scoring frozen before inference.

Primary success is readiness discrimination:

- ready cell: admit submission without unnecessary acquisition or mutation;
- not-ready cell: do not submit unchanged; identify a concrete true deficiency
  or make an admitted quality-improving repair.

Behavior change alone is insufficient. Actor action, artifact effect, exact
quality, carrier cost, and false-closure risk remain separate measures.
