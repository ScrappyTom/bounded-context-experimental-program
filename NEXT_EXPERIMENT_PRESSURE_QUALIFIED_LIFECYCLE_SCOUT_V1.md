# Next experiment: pressure-qualified lifecycle scout v1

Status: offline design direction only; no task freeze or GPU authorization

## Question

At a fresh ordinary trajectory's first authentic result-delivery overflow, does
the reactive runtime policy improve useful completion relative to the same
exact pre-pressure history without lifecycle intervention?

## Qualification

Accessible-world size is insufficient. A non-measured screening seed must
produce a requested exact result whose prospective delivery violates the frozen
prompt/reserve limit. The task may not be modified after screening. The screen
seed is not used in the measured comparison.

The preferred design freezes and forks the exact ordinary trajectory at that
boundary. Treatment and control are identical before pressure. Control reaches
the ordinary capacity endpoint. Treatment applies deterministic first-fit
relief and continues; reactive reentry is permitted only if the complete frozen
eligible relief class remains insufficient.

## Required Stage 0 corrections

- task-specific deterministic model-visible check diagnostics with exact raw
  custody;
- observation-to-candidate current/stale bindings;
- live qualification of a strict one-action interface;
- a minimum plausible successful path including effect uptake, review/repair,
  and closure;
- a prospective protocol-error allowance without retries;
- exact response/control reserve objects;
- independent candidate-bound evaluation/readiness for both arms;
- no treatment-only investigator-selected evidence.

The completed scout's 19/71 rejection rate and nine-call research ceiling are
nuisance evidence for design, not parameters to copy automatically.

## Interpretation

A positive result supports the complete reactive policy in the selected task
and seeds. It does not establish component necessity. A valid negative result
receives its frozen replication and routes the next mechanism from the first
new exact failure boundary. Survival alone is not useful completion, and false
closure is a loss.
