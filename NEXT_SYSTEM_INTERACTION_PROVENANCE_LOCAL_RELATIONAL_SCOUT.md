## Why this route exists

Complete global semantic replacement bundled preservation, new evidence,
coverage, conflict resolution, and bounded rewriting. Across E46, Cedar, and
Bluehaven it produced stale/no-op replacement, carrier pressure, error
capitalization, and unsupported completion.

E61 then showed the opposite extreme. Its source-local output was materially
safe, but lexical isolation rejected exact BRAMBLE relationships merely
because they named four other source objects. E62 showed that all sixteen
Meridian sources have such cross-source references.

E63 qualifies a middle apparatus model against ten exact historical fixtures:

- locality constrains mutation and attribution authority;
- a source-reported relationship may name a referent present in exact bound
  owner evidence;
- a source slot may not create another source's state;
- genuine synthesis uses a separate task-work record with its complete exact
  support set;
- source-version successors make prior claims stale without deleting lineage;
  and
- provenance transport never substitutes for semantic review.

E63 makes the route coherent. It does not show that Qwen can express the live
carrier or that the complete policy has positive utility.
