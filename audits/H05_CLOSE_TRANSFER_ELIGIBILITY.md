# H05 close-transfer eligibility audit

Date: 2026-08-22

## Intended question

Does the bounded progress-reconciliation package discriminate between:

1. a genuinely closure-ready state, where closure is appropriate; and
2. a genuinely defect-bearing state, where the useful next event is a concrete
   deficiency or repair rather than submission?

This is the correct transfer question after the H05 candidate-hash correction.
It is not enough to find another state where the package changes behavior.

## Prospective eligibility rules

A donor cell must have:

1. the same locked Qwen3.8 model/runtime or a separately declared transfer;
2. an exact reconstructable pre-decision packet;
3. stable task, evidence, candidate, and tool state;
4. no pending result or effect;
5. candidate readiness independently adjudicated and hash-bound before the
   maintenance output exists;
6. an authentic no-progress/reacquisition control phenotype;
7. capacity for control, maintenance, and treatment without changing the
   response reserve; and
8. no prior treated response at that same state.

A ready-versus-not-ready design requires at least one eligible cell of each
readiness class.

## Inventory result

### Recent standalone bounded-context series

No fresh closure-ready cell qualifies.

- The H05 effect-case candidate is defect-bearing and has already received the
  treatment. It is evidence, not a prospective cell.
- Other effect-case endpoints reuse the same strong-partial candidate or have
  missing/pending exact updates.
- Observation endpoints are incomplete acquisition/delivery states, not
  closure-ready states.
- No distinct exact packet combines an independently complete candidate with
  the repeated resident-reread phenotype and no pending update.

### Older Qwen3.8 research-state banks

The repository `E:\research-state-integration-2026-08-18` at commit
`b7e12ab21cec1ef8215e97dc9890924721651f1d` contains exact complete and
incomplete submitted code artifacts.

- Lease Pool seed 42 and 314159 trunks are independently complete (11/11), but
  their ordinary actors naturally checked and submitted. They do not expose the
  H05 no-progress/reacquisition phenotype.
- Token Buckets and Revision Store false-closure trunks are independently
  incomplete and naturally submitted after a narrow green check. They expose a
  verification/closure-evidence pressure, not the H05 stable-evidence reread
  pressure.

These banks could support a separately designed reflection/readiness study.
Using them as if they were a close transfer of H05 would change the trigger,
available evidence, task family, control behavior, and operational regime at
once.

## Decision

The planned close-transfer experiment is **ineligible**: eligible ready cells
`0`, eligible fresh not-ready cells `0`. No GPU call is authorized by this
design, and no standalone successor should be constructed merely to manufacture
the missing phenotype.

The older false-closure banks remain a possible future, explicitly new design:
candidate-bound progress reflection at a submission boundary, with independent
complete audit withheld from the actor but used for frozen scoring. That study
is not selected automatically here.
