## What changed

The refactored host is now common infrastructure for a complete interaction
comparison rather than the object under test. A separate `interaction_scout`
layer keeps semantic maintenance outside the host kernel and freezes two
fresh-trajectory Trellis configurations:

- `V0_EXACT_ARTIFACT`: exact evidence, common first-fit relief and reopen,
  exact incremental task artifacts, candidate/effect/check binding, bounded
  repair, recheck, and submission proposal;
- `V1_TEMPORARY_PROVENANCE_SCAFFOLD`: the same complete system plus charged,
  fallible anchored semantic maintenance only when exact source observations
  are actually externalized during construction. The scaffold is demoted when
  verification begins and has no readiness authority.

This is a compound whole-configuration contrast. A later behavioral difference
cannot be attributed to the scaffold alone because maintenance recency,
semantic state, artifact growth, action granularity, and later prompt/demand
all interact.

## Offline lifecycle result

Scripted provider-free paths in both configurations traversed:

```text
twelve-source acquisition
→ authentic repeated prompt pressure and first-fit relief
→ exact ledger and defect-bearing decision mutations
→ checkpoint at actor call 12
→ exact composite hydration and resume
→ verification transition
→ candidate-bound failing check
→ candidate/artifact/section-bound repair
→ effect delivery
→ current passing recheck
→ submission proposal
```

V0 used 19 actor/provider attempts, 317,677 serialized fixture tokens, nine
relief events, and four current-candidate state versions. It received no
semantic slot and paid no maintenance cost.

V1 used the same 19 actor attempts plus ten maintenance attempts, 359,098
serialized fixture tokens, ten relief events, four final register claims, and
the same four candidate versions. Its scaffold was exposed during construction
and replaced by a compact custody receipt in verification.

Every actor request exposed the canonical current-candidate slot. Verification
requests also exposed mechanically derived candidate/check currentness. A
truncated maintenance completion is charged but cannot be admitted; a tampered
register cannot hydrate from checkpoint. The full repository regression passes
290 tests, with Ruff and mypy clean.

These are apparatus facts. The scripted source claims and task actions are not
Qwen behavior, do not establish semantic utility, and do not promote an
architecture.

## Frozen first live tranche

The only selected GPU operation is a twelve-actor-call checkpoint tranche for
each fresh configuration, in V0-then-V1 order:

- at most 24 actor calls total;
- at most 12 treatment maintenance calls;
- at most 36 provider calls;
- at most 900,000 serialized tokens;
- one attempt per call and zero retries;
- a fresh server process per configuration;
- mandatory literal transcript review after each checkpoint.

The host records exact state, recurrence, and effects but does not decide that
the actor is looping or ready. Codex reviews what was visible, what demand was
new or repeated, what entered the exact artifact, whether effects/checks crossed
a later decision, and where the whole system's bottleneck moved. Continuation
requires separate authorization; changing the policy starts a new experiment.
