## Offline qualification

Nineteen tests pass. The provider-free full-loop fixture executes both systems
through:

```text
read
→ exact delivery
→ positive-savings relief
→ semantic integration
→ actor-visible state/effect
→ incremental candidate effect
→ check
→ repair

→ current recheck
→ submission proposal
```

The fixture proves the intended coupling difference:

- detached maintenance produces `semantic_state_effect` and leaves the
  composite candidate unchanged;
- coupled maintenance produces `candidate_effect` and changes the exact
  composite candidate;
- ordinary actor section work changes either candidate;
- a later effect marks the prior check stale; and
- rechecking the exact current candidate restores current status.

The relief regression fixes E40's apparatus defect. The selector
prospectively renders each receipt, skips equal- and negative-savings
candidates, protects a pending result, chooses the oldest strictly positive
candidate, and applies at most one substitution before the complete system is
rerendered. Maintenance/artifact growth is therefore included in the next
feasibility decision.

## Evaluator and readiness

The candidate identity is a deterministic manifest hash over both deliverable
files. Every mutation effect names the prior and current composite hashes and
the changed file. Checks bind to that exact composite hash and surface current
or stale status mechanically.

The actor-visible evaluator checks structure, source breadth, requirement
coverage, and mechanical content gates. It never grants final readiness:
mechanically complete candidates remain `not_adjudicated` until independent,
condition-blinded semantic review. Submission remains a proposal, not proof of
success.
