# Cross-study information-economics episode ledger — analysis plan

Date: 2026-08-22

Status: selected offline analysis; no GPU authorization requested

## Question

Across the completed Qwen3.8 studies, under which observed configurations did
an information operation improve physical operability, decision quality,
artifact quality, or closure—and where did its cost or failure migrate?

This is not a model experiment and not a retrospective search for one winning
prompt. It is an evidence-integration step required before spending more GPU
calls on mechanisms already represented in the donor program.

## Unit of analysis

One row is one frozen boundary × condition × measured continuation, not one
whole experiment headline.

Each row must bind:

- repository, commit, run, cell, model profile, task, candidate, and evaluator;
- operational regime: acquisition, construction, repair, review, or closure;
- exact pressure or opportunity boundary;
- model-visible resident configuration and externally recoverable state;
- operation, trigger, owner, and treatment delta;
- prompt, completion, cache, latency, maintenance, and recovery costs;
- next action, later actions, exact effects, and artifact-quality change;
- whether information demand was reduced, redirected, expanded, or deferred;
- failure migration and endpoint; and
- semantic/audit confidence plus known confounds.

## Initial source families

1. current standalone context reduction, reentry, exact-state, note, digest,
   residency, and progress-state studies;
2. older virtualized-residency and current-object studies;
3. older phase-state, longitudinal reentry, and scheduled-handoff studies;
4. older complete-audit false-closure and submission-bound studies; and
5. older model-authored review and semantic-continuity studies.

Every imported result must be read from a pinned Git object and retain its
original claim limit. Aggregate experiment prose is insufficient when exact
cell records exist.

## Derived comparisons

The first analysis should test only descriptive, predeclared comparisons:

- mechanical capacity restored versus useful action produced;
- exact recoverability versus stable semantic co-residency;
- producer/carrier cost versus avoided later recovery;
- maintenance reachability versus maintenance selection quality;
- fresh reentry versus transcript accumulation by phase and artifact quality;
- exact verification versus model-authored review at closure;
- behavior change versus actual artifact utility; and
- immediate benefit versus later churn, reacquisition, or false closure.

Do not pool heterogeneous task scores as if they shared one scale. Normalize
only within a task/evaluator basis and keep criterion-level changes where
available.

## Deliverables

- a schema and field dictionary;
- pinned source and materialization receipts;
- an episode table with explicit missingness;
- deterministic validation and duplicate-boundary detection;
- descriptive tables by regime and operation;
- a contradiction/overlap report against the current evidence ledger;
- a short list of genuinely untested pressure × operation cells; and
- a routing recommendation that may select no GPU successor.

## Stop rules

Stop rather than infer when candidate/task/evaluator identities cannot be
reconciled, when cell-level costs cannot be separated from aggregate totals, or
when two records describe the same trajectory inconsistently.

Do not use outcome-aware feature selection to manufacture a policy. Do not
train a router, rank representations globally, or treat correlation as causal
effect. The immediate goal is to prevent redundant experiments and expose the
smallest genuinely unresolved comparison.
