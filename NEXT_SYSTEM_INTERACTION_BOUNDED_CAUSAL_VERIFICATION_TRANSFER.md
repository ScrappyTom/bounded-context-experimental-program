Date: 2026-08-27

## Question

Can a complete bounded lifecycle preserve current state plus the small amount
of unresolved exact causality needed to turn feedback into non-repeating repair
and correct closure?

The unit is a complete system operating over time:

```text
pressured evidence ingress
→ temporary anchored semantic scaffold × exact incremental artifact
→ actor-selected verification
→ current check × exact causal continuity
→ uniquely bound repair
→ effect uptake → current recheck
→ independent readiness → correct closure
```

This is not an isolated causal-tail prompt test.

## Evidence gate

E77 audits 157 sealed actor calls in ten cells and four independent worlds.
Action-transport failure plus functional recurrence occurs in Architecture,
Solace, and Orchard. Rejected mutation recurrence occurs specifically in
Architecture and Orchard. Cedar and admitted Solace patch/check loops serve as
controls.

The proposed host-mechanical frame costs at most 1,297 tokenizer tokens over
audited endpoints. A candidate-/artifact-/section-hash-bound repair costs 461
tokens in the Orchard fixture and reaches provider-free effect uptake and a
current recheck. Neither result establishes actor utility.

## Compared viable systems

Both systems share:

* exact external custody and first-fit relief;
* source access and exact reopen;
* temporary anchored semantic construction scaffold;
* exact incremental task artifact;
* actor-selected verification transition;
* current candidate/check projection;
* uniquely bound section repair;
* identical evaluator, readiness, resources, and stop rules.

V0 replaces current verification state as new events arrive. An unresolved
rejection may leave the compact projection when a later observation arrives.

V1 retains a bounded host-derived causal projection containing latest action/
outcome, unresolved rejected mutation until candidate change, exact recurrence
inside the current candidate epoch, and full-history handle.

The host does not choose a repair, infer intent, or authorize closure.

## Outcomes

Primary outcomes remain artifact relationship fidelity, correct repair, effect
uptake, current recheck, readiness discrimination, closure, and useful
completion. Secondary outcomes include rejected-action recovery, exact action
recurrence, induced evidence demand, prompt/response cost, cache/latency, frame
cost, and failure migration.

A V1 advantage supports the complete causal-current lifecycle in that
configuration, not the necessity of every field. A tie or V0 advantage leaves
the frame mechanically feasible but economically unearned.

Do not tune Orchard, add a semantic progress cue, or let recurrence force a
repair or submission.

No GPU run is authorized by this plan.
