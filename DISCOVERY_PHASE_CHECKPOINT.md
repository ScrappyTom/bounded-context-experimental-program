# Discovery-phase checkpoint and first breadth tranche

Date: 2026-08-23

Status: tranche completed and sealed; prospective design retained for audit

## Decision in one sentence

Use the current exact-custody machinery as an observation instrument, then run
one small Qwen3.8 reconnaissance tranche across known, independently scored
task geometries rather than another treatment series at the Ceiba pressure
boundary.

The tranche is not intended to identify a winning memory format. It is intended
to observe which part of this pipeline becomes limiting in different worlds:

```text
accessible world
-> discovery and exact observation
-> relationship formation
-> relation-to-action binding
-> action organization
-> legal expression and admission
-> world/candidate effect
-> current feedback
-> repair or closure
```

Exact source custody, history, candidate versions, and evaluation surround the
pipeline. They do not by themselves perform any of its semantic functions.

## Measured disposition

The authorized tranche ran from freeze
`f3092c73065353d5fbdb4c3646632b6586c7e50b` under run ID
`2026-08-23-capability-terrain-v0`. The exact result is published at commit
`ea8f4cfa5888998d6193f57316e9cd41cf1639b6` and interpreted in
`DISCOVERY_TRANCHE_RESULT.md`.

It used 39 of the authorized 42 actor calls, one provider attempt per call, and
zero retries. Nineteen responses were rejected; no pressure event occurred; D2
and D5 produced strong-partial artifacts and closed prematurely; D6 repeated
D2's premature closure from exact current state; and no cell achieved correct
useful completion.

The tranche therefore supplies a low-occupancy reference for
expression/admission, relation-to-action, verification coverage, and readiness.
It does not answer how those functions interact with context lifecycle because
pressure relief, semantic persistence, and incremental work did not co-activate
in a matched design. The successor direction is governed by
`SYSTEM_INTERACTION_EXPLORATION.md`, not by one follow-up per observed boundary.

The remainder of this document preserves the prospectively frozen questions,
portfolio, gates, and stopping rules. It is not rewritten around the observed
outcomes and does not authorize a repaired cell or successor tranche.

## 1. Current research question

The active question is:

> Across materially different bounded tasks, where does useful work stop, and
> which functions already work under ordinary exact history and ordinary
> artifact operations?

E36 answered a narrower question well. First-fit demotion repeatedly kept exact
evidence entering a fresh large-world trajectory. The decision-level audit now
shows why another receipt-only extension is not the best next use of inference:

- eleven complete sources were observed over time;
- only a small moving tail remained literal;
- no model-authored relationship or partial artifact persisted;
- S12 had not crossed and S14 had not been requested;
- the only mutation operation asked for a complete 1,800–2,000-word charter;
  and
- the call budget ended before synthesis, effect uptake, checking, or closure
  became observable.

That configuration does not isolate forgetting, acquisition stopping, model
inability, or a missing memory layer. It shows that information ingress,
semantic integration, work-product persistence, and decision bandwidth form a
coupled system.

The sister Qwen3.6 program broadens the same picture on coding tasks. It shows
that explicit relationship representation may still fail to constrain code;
action organization may determine whether a candidate is materialized without
determining correctness; checks may run without discriminating defects; and
correct artifacts may still fail to close. Those are cross-profile functional
analogues, not Qwen3.8 replications.

## 2. Architectural commitments versus earned evidence

### Earned and retained

- exact task, source, candidate, action, observation, effect, and history
  custody;
- exact version, candidate, and reopen bindings;
- truthful result/effect delivery accounting;
- hard context, response, call, token, and authorization ceilings;
- deterministic admission and externally reproducible action effects;
- candidate-bound checks with explicit current/stale status where applicable;
- independent artifact/readiness evaluation; and
- deterministic first-fit relief as a pressure instrument when an exact
  pending result otherwise cannot cross.

These are observation, safety, and governance functions.

### Still hypotheses, not architecture

- a stable host-selected exact working set;
- semantic notes, digests, ledgers, or progress state;
- a permanent four-field state object;
- model-owned eviction or workspace negotiation;
- automatic phase discovery;
- child/curator/distiller/composer roles;
- a universal action schema;
- decomposition as a default; and
- any routing policy that decides which semantic operation is needed.

The first tranche will not promote or optimize any of these.

## 3. Validity requirements versus solution-shaped apparatus

### Required for validity

- exact immutable starting worlds and task text;
- one frozen Qwen3.8 model/runtime/template/sampler profile;
- exact model-visible inputs and outputs;
- no hidden retry or repaired model action;
- deterministic file/action admission;
- every result and effect marked acquired, delivered, or undelivered;
- hard per-cell and tranche budgets;
- exact current-candidate bindings on reads, effects, and checks;
- frozen task-derived evaluators and readiness decisions; and
- preservation of null, malformed, harmful, and incomplete cells.

### Not required merely to make a design look complete

- a model-authored state schema;
- a semantic router;
- host relevance labels;
- a task-phase controller;
- a generalized context-object ontology;
- a fixed memory hierarchy;
- a per-cell publication-grade release package before signal is known; or
- an automatic successor when a probe is interesting.

The exploratory records must still preserve raw requests, responses, actions,
results, candidates, evaluator outputs, and a compact replay receipt. Formal
multi-seed banks and full independent replay packages belong to confirmation.

## 4. Competing live explanations

At least six explanations remain materially plausible and can coexist:

1. **Discovery/selection limitation.** Relevant information is not found or the
   wrong exact object is read.
2. **Relationship limitation.** Relevant facts are observed but their
   conjunction, precedence, qualification, or causal relation is not formed.
3. **Binding limitation.** The relation is explicitly represented but does not
   constrain the artifact or mutation.
4. **Organization/expression limitation.** A useful operation is intended but
   scoped, sequenced, or serialized in a way that never reaches a legal effect.
5. **Persistence/economics limitation.** The model can integrate locally, but
   the action surface does not let understanding accumulate economically in
   exact work before source turnover.
6. **Feedback/control limitation.** Effects are visible, but checks are stale,
   nondiscriminating, misleading, or overtrusted; repair and closure then
   diverge from artifact quality.

Task geometry, call allowance, response allocation, and interface semantics
can create or hide each boundary. No single cell can assign all causality to
the model.

## 5. First discovery tranche

### Portfolio contract

The proposed tranche contains six trajectories across five source/candidate
lineages. It uses one Qwen3.8 profile and at most 42 actor calls. Every valid,
invalid, harmful, null, or activated cell consumes its slot and calls. There is
no automatic replacement or second wave.

The task worlds are deliberately known-geometry fixtures already frozen and
scored in other programs. This reduces evaluator invention and supplies
cross-profile triangulation. It also makes the portfolio enriched rather than
representative. No prevalence estimate or “fresh task” success rate may be
reported.

All task text, source/candidate manifests, action contracts, evaluators,
readiness rules, run order, seed, and model profile must be materialized and
frozen in a new Stage 0 commit before a GPU request. Existing sister outcomes
must not be shown to the actor.

The task fixtures intentionally differ in world structure and, where required,
in task-native action affordances. Stage 0 must declare the shared core actions
and every task-specific addition. The common model profile supports
cross-situation observation; it does not turn the portfolio into a controlled
effect estimate for task type, action interface, or domain. Rates are reported
per cell and are not pooled.

| Order | Probe | Source/candidate lineage | Primary capability question | Calls |
|---:|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | D1 large-world navigation prefix | Q3 XL large-world memo task and frozen atlas | Does the actor move from a large mechanical hierarchy to discriminating search/read behavior, or spend the narrow horizon enumerating structure? | 4 |
| 2 | D2 evidence-bound paper revision | `paper-revision-v1` fixture | Can exact evidence and editorial qualifications become a coherent multi-section edit, and which represented requirements fail to bind? | 8 |
| 3 | D3 ordinary-success calibration | `coverage-coalescing` fixture | What does clean discovery→mutation→check→closure look like on a task previously solvable by ordinary harnesses? | 8 |
| 4 | D4 rule-dense construction | `device-event-folding` fixture | Does a compact but conjunctive protocol become exact code, and where do formation, binding, expression, feedback, or closure split? | 9 |
| 5 | D5 action/effect/repair | `deployment-wave-planning` fixture | Can the actor organize a response into admitted bounded work, bind the effect to the current candidate, use a discriminating check, and repair or close? | 8 |
| 6 | D6 exact fresh review/resumption | first eligible candidate from D2–D5 under the rule below | What survives when a fresh actor receives exact current state and current feedback without the construction chronology? | 5 |

Maximum: 42 actor calls. The tranche remains within the roadmap's four-to-six
trajectory and sixty-call caps.

The selection is purposive: one oversized discovery situation, one
relationship-rich research edit, one previously ordinary-successful coding
calibration, two different rule/action/effect coding situations, and one
conditional fresh-state handoff. This samples contrasts that the current map
needs; it does not estimate how often any boundary occurs in natural work.

D2–D5 are end-to-end lifecycle observations, not three-to-five-call
continuations from an already-formed action. Their horizons must be justified
in Stage 0 by a task-specific minimum path through discovery/construction,
effect uptake, evaluation, and closure plus the declared one-malformed-action
slack. A cell whose geometry does not support that path within its listed
budget is ineligible; its unused slot is not replaced.

### D4 — rule-dense construction

Source instrument:

### D5 — action/effect/repair

Source instrument:

- `deployment-wave-planning__s3476` from the Experiment 107 task-world
  family;
- exact dependency-wave contract, starting code, tests, and eight-boundary
  external grader; and
- a new isolated recursive-hash manifest.

The previous Qwen3.6 study exposed a response-allocation boundary near 4,096
tokens. D5 does not increase the shared allowance. It preserves both exact
bounded patch and whole-file mutation as ordinary legal choices, so the actor
can organize work without a host-selected solution. A Stage 0 known-good path
must prove that at least one legal solution can be expressed within the shared
allowance.

Record whether the actor chooses another read, a bounded patch, a whole-file

### D6 — exact fresh review/resumption

D6 is prospectively conditional and still consumes the sixth slot.

After D2–D5 complete, select the first cell in that fixed order that produced
an admitted changed candidate. Apply its already frozen external evaluator
without consulting the model response. D6 receives:

- the exact original task and any immutable task input that was literal for
  every donor decision;
- the exact current candidate and version;
- the latest current candidate-bound check result, if one exists;
- explicit mechanical current/stale labels;
- exact handles to prior observations, but no prior observation body other
  than the separately declared latest current check; and
- no construction chronology, semantic note, readiness label, or prior final.

The accessible external world and task-native source tools remain the same as
in the donor. Reentry cannot inject an investigator-selected source, an unread
source body, a hidden grader finding, or a gold patch.

The fresh actor may inspect, patch/replace, check, and submit under a five-call
ceiling when its exact actual packet fits. If no D2–D5 candidate changed, D6 is
a declared null. If a changed candidate exists but the exact actual packet
exceeds the frozen prompt ceiling even after the permitted mechanical
projection, D6 is a declared `capacity_ineligible` zero-call branch. Both null
conditions consume the slot and are not replaced by an invented backup.

This is a same-lineage descriptive handoff assay, not independent recurrence,
a matched control, or a causal chronology ablation. Its question is simply
what the fresh actor inspects, reacquires, changes, verifies, and closes from
the frozen exact-state package, and what information appears necessary but
absent. Any effect belongs to the complete branch-specific package.

Before D2 begins, Stage 0 must freeze and qualify the renderer, action surface,
ordinary known-good and prospectively declared stress packets for every D2,
D3, D4, and D5 donor lineage, the actual-packet capacity gate, and both null
paths. This is not a claim that every legally admitted pathological UTF-8 file
has a five-call handoff envelope. Exact candidate and observation identities
remain run-derived inputs to that frozen renderer; branch logic and packet
selection may not be repaired after the donor outcome is known.

## 6. Action and evaluation geometry preflight

Before freezing the tranche, Stage 0 must prove for each full-task probe:

1. the initial candidate fails at least one evaluator requirement;
2. a known-good candidate exists and passes the frozen evaluator; where the
   evaluator includes a semantic readiness rubric, Stage 0 must retain a
   candidate/task/evidence-bound criterion-level adjudication rather than
   infer readiness from a narrower machine grade;
3. the known-good path is expressible through at least one legal action sequence
   within the response and call ceilings, with a documented minimum-path
   decomposition for discovery/construction, effect uptake, check, repair, and
   closure as applicable;
4. one ordinary malformed action does not make all useful completion
   mechanically impossible;
5. mutation effects and checks are exact candidate-bound observations;
6. raw evaluator output is preserved, while any model-visible projection is
   deterministic and stable over volatile UUID/time/path values;
7. the evaluator distinguishes artifact correctness from submission/closure;
   and
8. no treatment-only source, relevance judgment, gold patch, or readiness cue
   enters the actor context.

D1 is exempt from a complete-artifact path because its claim is explicitly
limited to four-decision discovery/selection behavior.

## 7. Endpoint coding for the tranche

Every cell will receive a literal sequence code, potentially with more than
one boundary:

- relevant information not discovered;
- discovered but not made literal;
- literal evidence not behaviorally used;
- governing relationship not formed;
- governing relationship represented but not bound into action;
- intended operation formed but poorly organized;
- target operation selected but no legal expression reached the environment;
- candidate admitted with a governing qualification lost;
- effect occurred but was not taken up as current state;
- check ran but did not discriminate the defect;
- discriminating feedback arrived but repair failed;
- artifact correct but testing/repair continued;
- artifact incorrect but fluent closure occurred;
- response continuation or reentry was absent, unused, or ineligible;
- resource budget ended before the capability became observable; or
- useful completion.

For each code, retain at least two plausible explanations when the literal
trajectory does not identify cause.

## 8. Cost and stopping rules

Report a vector, not one scalar utility:

- prompt/completion and cached/uncached tokens;
- actor calls and rejected actions;
- bytes read and reread;
- exact objects resident/external;
- mutations and effect uptake;
- checks and their discrimination;
- artifact criterion results;
- closure/readiness alignment; and
- wall time.

No cell is extended because it looks close. No second seed follows a good,
bad, or surprising result. A later confirmation is eligible only after a
practically important boundary recurs in an independent world and the proposed
comparison can adjudicate downstream quality.

One exception is permitted for safety governance: a single severe
authorization breach, irreversible effect, false closure, or custody/integrity
failure may trigger one bounded forensic or safety-confirmation study. That
exception cannot promote an architecture and does not waive owner review.

## 9. What will not be built in this wave

- no new first-fit order or longer Ceiba horizon;
- no digest, global note, progress state, coverage ledger, or relation surface;
- no model-managed eviction;
- no role router or automatic phase detector;
- no generic child-agent protocol;
- no learned memory/controller;
- no task-specific rescue after a valid failure; and
- no production runtime abstraction beyond the thin exact workbench needed to
  run the six declared situations.

## 10. Owner-review boundary (historical pre-run contract)

This checkpoint selects a bounded portfolio but does not authorize model calls.
Before GPU use, return a Stage 0 package containing:

- exact task/source/candidate/evaluator manifests for D1–D5;
- D6 activation and rendering receipts for all four possible donor branches
  and both null paths, including each branch's action surface, ordinary and
  stress packet counts, and the exact live capacity rule;
- the common model/runtime/profile lock;
- exact action schemas and known-good feasibility receipts;
- fixed run order, seed, calls, and total token ceiling;
- blinded or deterministic evaluator identities and readiness fields; and
- the resulting frozen commit.

Owner review is then required for the single finite tranche. Any change to the
task set, a repair after observing a cell, an additional trajectory, or a

The code probes execute model-authored JavaScript on disposable candidate and
evaluator copies with exact pre/post integrity checks and timeout tree cleanup.
That protects experimental custody but is not an operating-system sandbox.
The exact GPU authorization must explicitly accept the host-permission risk;
otherwise D3-D5 are not authorized to run.

## Evidence-status summary

- Exact mechanical pressure relief is qualified as infrastructure.
- E36's cumulative integration failure remains configuration-level and
  causally unresolved.
- The completed breadth tranche adds Qwen3.8 evidence that action expression
  can dominate at low occupancy and that substantial relation-to-action work,
  current-check uptake, and closure can still leave material requirements
  unsatisfied.
- Premature closure now appears in two independent tranche lineages, while D6
  shows that exact current-state reentry can preserve the closure disposition
  without correcting readiness.
- Exact partial work as durable cognition and genuine decomposed reintegration
  remain largely untested.
- The completed tranche is an enriched capability reconnaissance, not an
  architecture bake-off, prevalence sample, or benchmark.
- Its capability labels are endpoint codes, not an instruction to isolate each
  component. The next checkpoint must study their interaction inside viable
  pressure-active configurations.
