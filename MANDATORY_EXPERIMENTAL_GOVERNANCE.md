# Mandatory experimental governance for whole-method scout v0

Date: 2026-08-22
Status: common measurement and custody contract; not a runtime treatment

## Separation from runtime

The evaluation ledger, replay checks, readiness adjudication, and custody
receipts apply identically to control and treatment. They are not counted as an
actor benefit unless a future experiment explicitly makes evaluation visible
to the actor. Scout v0 does not.

## Common evidence parity

Common across conditions:

- authoritative task and exact accessible world;
- source/object availability, tools, and permissions;
- task-authored phase contracts;
- exogenous evidence delivered at phase transitions;
- model, runtime, template, sampler, context, response reserve, attempts;
- action admission and evaluator.

Arm-specific after behavior diverges:

- actor-requested reads and their observations;
- mutations and effects;
- current model-visible residency; and
- ordinary endogenous chronology.

Treatment reentry cannot add post-outcome evidence or investigator-selected
semantic help.

## Artifact and readiness adjudication

Before assigning task utility, bind every judgment to:

- exact candidate/tree and artifact file-hash manifest;
- authoritative task-spec hash;
- rubric/evaluator identity and hash;
- evidence-manifest hash and coverage;
- criterion-level dispositions;
- explicit `ready`, `not_ready`, or `not_adjudicated` state;
- blocking requirements; and
- independent, inherited, reconciled, or superseded provenance.

Search prior candidate/task adjudication lineage before declaring improvement
or closure. Unexplained conflicts stop interpretation. Submission, fewer calls,
reduced reading, or lower token use do not establish useful completion.

Evaluation is condition-blinded where feasible. Code tasks require both tests
and task-derived semantic criteria. Research/writing tasks require exact source
grounding and preservation of material qualifications.

## Joint primary disposition

```text
useful_completion =
    artifact meets frozen acceptance criteria
    AND closure/readiness is correct
    AND final relevant effects crossed a model boundary
        OR were independently reconciled
```

Report separately:

- artifact quality;
- closure correctness;
- effect uptake;
- capacity survival;
- calls, tokens, cache, and latency;
- reopen churn and phase-transition cost; and
- first failure migration.

Do not collapse these into an unearned scalar utility score.

## Replication rule

The second frozen seed runs after any eligible, mechanically valid,
policy-relevant, interpretable first comparison—including treatment loss, harm,
control win, false closure, high cost, or a changed failure mode.

Seed 2 may be skipped only for:

- experiment ineligibility;
- apparatus/runtime failure invalidating the design;
- structurally nondiagnostic task geometry; or
- safety/custody failure.

One seed merely failing to activate pressure is not by itself structural
nonactivation.

## Required Stage 0 package

Before GPU authorization, freeze for each task:

1. task hash, initial world/candidate manifest, evidence manifest, tool schema,
   and model/runtime lock;
2. evaluator/rubric identity, criterion-level scoring, evidence coverage,
   readiness rule, and candidate-hash reconciliation;
3. phase names, common boundary event, common exogenous payload, treatment
   reentry admissibility, and control transition rendering;
4. eligible result classes, frozen scan order, exact receipt format,
   stop-at-first-feasible algorithm, pending-result protection, ordinary
   response reserve, and exact transition reserve;
5. call, transition, token, retry, wall-clock, and exhaustion contracts; and
6. a causal interpretation stating that this is a compound policy comparison.

No GPU execution is authorized until both task geometries and evaluators pass
this offline qualification.
