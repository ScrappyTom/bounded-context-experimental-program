## What the experiment teaches

Supported locally:

- exact custody and receipts did not preserve action-ready consumption in V0;
- the V1 interaction changed the actor from full catalog replay to construction;
- exact task artifacts can carry cumulative work after raw sources leave;
- lossy semantic selection can become durable artifact error;
- append-only mutation effects can consume the verification opportunity even
  though the current artifact already contains their cumulative outcome.

Not supported:

- useful completion;
- sufficient semantic fidelity;
- verification, repair, recheck, or correct closure;
- register-only causality; or
- architecture promotion.

The exact E96 checkpoint route is closed. The next work is an offline lifecycle
design: preserve exact effect custody and delivery auditability while keeping
only the current effect/currentness projection model-facing once its outcome is
already represented by the current candidate.
