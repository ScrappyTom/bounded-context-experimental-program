## What Stage 0 establishes

The fresh task is an independent Northstar multi-region event-ingestion
migration from LegacyQueue to StreamCore. Fourteen generated and exactly pinned
sources contain 146,617 bytes (61,500 offline-tokenizer tokens). The initial
actor prompt is only 2,863 tokens. The design therefore does not mistake total
accessible-world size for realized model-facing pressure.

The task requires two exact artifacts:

- `EVIDENCE_INTEGRATION_LEDGER.md`; and
- `BOUNDED_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION.md`.

The decision has eight exact sections, a 1,500–2,100-word bound, frozen source
coverage, and twelve task-derived requirements spanning identity, retry and
deduplication, idempotent effects, schema evolution, EU routing, outage
buffering, per-tenant latency, reconciliation, regional capacity, and
retention/audit controls.

The viable whole-system comparison is:

- `D0_DETACHED`: accepted bounded integration remains a non-authoritative
  sidecar and does not mutate the exact candidate; and
- `A1_COUPLED`: the same accepted integration becomes the exact, versioned,
  task-required evidence ledger and emits candidate effects.

Both systems otherwise retain actor-selected one-range and batch ingress,
exact custody and reopen, strictly positive deterministic first-fit relief,
maintenance, ordinary candidate work, version-bound effects and checks,
repair, reactive reentry, closure proposal, and external candidate-bound
evaluation. The treatment is thus the coupling among semantic integration,
durable task work, feedback, and later action—not a standalone note or patch
component.

## Authentic activation gate

The next gate is an 18-call ordinary-chronology screen with no relief,
maintenance, coupling, sidecar, or reentry. A boundary qualifies only when:

1. the pending result is an exact source observation;
2. at least four earlier source observations crossed model-decision
   boundaries;
3. the candidate is unchanged;
4. no check or submission occurred;
5. the prospective prompt exceeds the 20,992-token prompt allowance; and
6. an independent post-run audit proves that at least one strictly positive
   first-fit substitution admits the same protected pending result.

The runner, audit, token reconstruction, seal, exact source/result bindings,
and handoff renderer are frozen. The measured D0/A1 study remains ineligible
unless the screen produces and commits this exact boundary.

## Candidate-bound evaluation and readiness

The mechanical evaluator is bound to the exact task lock, world specification,
and candidate files. Stable diagnostics retain raw exact results externally
while exposing deterministic task-criterion projections.

Semantic readiness is separately bound to the exact candidate, task, evidence,
rubric, and blocking requirements. Readiness requires all R01–R12, no
unsupported claim or forbidden shortcut, no blocker, and a current final check
for the current candidate. A mechanical pass alone yields
`not_adjudicated`; submission never changes readiness.

## Post-construction decision budget

The budget is event-contingent rather than a fixed phase clock.

- The actor has at most 22 calls to cross a mechanical construction milestone:
  exact title, all eight headings, at least 1,100 words, and at least eight
  cited sources.
- Crossing that milestone allocates exactly eight additional actor calls,
  capped at 30 total calls per cell.
- The clean terminal path needs four decisions: receive the construction
  effect and check; receive the check and repair; receive the repair effect and
  recheck; receive the current recheck and close.
- Four additional decisions cover ordinary action errors or an extra repair
  cycle without making replication outcome-dependent.

Failure to construct by call 22 and exhaustion of the tail have distinct
terminal dispositions. Neither forces submission or injects a semantic
progress cue.
