## Why this audit was necessary

E69 established the exact outcomes of the Solace comparison, but its shorthand
description compressed W0 into “fourteen reopens and no construction.” That was
mechanically correct only if construction meant an **admitted candidate
mutation**. It was not a faithful description of the final two actor turns.

E70 reads every one of the 34 sealed provider turns and records, for each call:

- exact result bodies and receipts visible before the call;
- register and candidate version;
- literal action or maintenance output;
- admitted semantic/register/artifact change;
- information incorporated, omitted, distorted, or lost;
- the next observable demand shift;
- alternative explanations and confidence;
- exact transcript and candidate evidence paths.

It adds no model calls, changes no sealed bytes, and does not regrade either
candidate.

## Revised causal interpretation

The exact treatment remains causally active because W0 and L1 shared the frozen
fork and L1 alone received anchored-provenance maintenance. But the treatment
does not identify one isolated mechanism. It jointly changed:

```text
resident semantic residue
+ recent maintenance messages
+ exact evidence-ledger state
+ actor action selection
+ construction timing and granularity
+ completion/action transport exposure
```

The useful contrast is therefore:

```text
W0
rich exact ledger
-> late all-at-once construction
-> two length-ended unadmitted drafts

L1
anchored residue + rich exact ledger
-> early section-sized admitted construction
-> global consolidation
-> strong-partial artifact
```

This is a representation × work-externalization × action-transport interaction.
It is stronger than a token-capacity story and narrower than “the register made
Qwen understand.”

## Economic reconciliation

L1 still consumed 137,793 fewer serialized tokens than W0, or 38.5 percent.
That is a descriptive whole-trajectory difference, not a clean estimate of
register efficiency. W0 spent 8,192 completion tokens on two rejected drafts,
and the arms stopped at different lifecycle states.

The economic lesson is that semantic accumulation can change not only what the
actor knows, but **how and when it attempts to externalize work**. A bounded
semantic treatment may save or waste tokens indirectly through its effect on
action geometry. Carrier cost, construction granularity, admission reliability,
and downstream artifact quality must therefore be measured together.

## Roadmap effect

The planned artifact-only versus artifact-plus-register verification reentry is
still a legitimate marginal-value test *after construction*. It can ask whether
the frozen register remains useful once much of its meaning has been capitalized
into exact artifacts.

It cannot explain the W0/L1 acquisition-to-construction divergence. That is a
separate interaction boundary involving representation, construction timing,
incremental work actions, and transport. The post-construction ablation must not
be reported as isolating why W0 failed.

No new GPU run is authorized by this audit.
