# Next offline reconciliation — evidence maturity × semantic completeness

Date: 2026-08-25

Status: recommended program-level audit after E56; not yet executed; no GPU
work or successor treatment authorized

## Why stop before another runner

E56 changes the conceptual map. The B1 carrier was small enough and live-
expressible, but its first complete ledger replacement was invoked with only a
partial exact evidence basis. The model filled the remaining task state with
unsupported source attributions. The later broader-evidence replacement
passed.

This raises a recurrence question that should be answered across existing
evidence before creating another task or prompt:

> When bounded semantic maintenance is required to express a complete task
> state, how does the maturity of its exact evidence basis affect source
> fidelity, admission, downstream work, and cost?

## Exact donor population

Audit only already sealed maintenance invocations with exact inputs and
outputs from the artifact-coupling line:

- E44/E46 original detached/coupled interaction;
- E52 Cedar transfer interaction;
- E56 Bluehaven initialization and replacement qualification; and
- earlier expression-qualification cases only as transport references, not as
  trajectory utility evidence.

Every donor must be bound to repository, commit, run ID, candidate/task lock,
input result IDs, exact prior state, and output hash. Rejected content remains
rejected.

## Per-invocation reconstruction

Record mechanically where possible:

- exact sources present in newly supplied observations;
- sources represented in the prior accepted semantic state;
- union of evidence available to that maintenance call;
- task requirements the output format demanded it address;
- output source identifiers and requirement identifiers;
- identifiers outside the exact allowlist;
- output size, finish reason, admission, and rejection reason;
- semantic claims whose cited source can be checked against exact donor bytes;
- candidate effect and whether it crossed a later actor boundary;
- next actor action, later work reuse, repair, and check currency;
- provider, token, cache, and decision-opportunity cost.

## Systems classifications

Classify without repairing outputs:

- **grounded partial state** — leaves unsupported requirements explicitly
  unresolved without false provenance;
- **grounded complete state** — exact evidence supports the expressed task
  coverage;
- **identity-only future reference** — names an unavailable source solely as a
  reopen/acquisition target;
- **unsupported semantic completion** — asserts task substance under evidence
  absent from the call;
- **wrong source binding** — exact cited source contradicts or does not govern
  the asserted relationship;
- **carrier failure** — truncation, budget, syntax, or heading failure; and
- **downstream capitalization** — admitted semantic content becomes exact work
  or materially shapes later action.

The classification must distinguish task-derived obligations from source-
grounded findings. A task statement can require an assay or review without
proving which source establishes it.

## Decision rule after the audit

If unsupported completion recurs across at least two independent task worlds,
the next fresh interaction scout may compare complete policies such as:

```text
M1_EVIDENCE_MATURE
semantic replacement fires only at a prospectively mechanical
evidence-maturity event; exact relief and ordinary work continue otherwise

W1_DIRECT_WORK
no separate semantic provider; the actor externalizes interpretation through
exact task-native work under the same pressure and verification substrate
```

That would test timing, semantic persistence, task work, effects, and
verification jointly. It must use a fresh world and cannot reuse Bluehaven as
a tuned measured task.

If E56 is isolated to its R/S identifier geometry or qualification prompt,
do not promote an evidence-maturity controller. Record the local hazard and
select the next broad scout from a different recurring interaction boundary.

## Prohibitions

The audit may not:

- admit or repair rejected model content;
- infer that omitted exact evidence was internally remembered;
- treat same-world cases as independent recurrence;
- turn source-ID absence into semantic irrelevance;
- relax provenance rules because a task-level statement resembles a claim;
- manufacture a new B1 run on Bluehaven; or
- authorize any provider call.

This is a program-routing audit, not a new component qualification.
