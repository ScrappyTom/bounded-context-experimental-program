## What changed after Northstar

Northstar permitted a three-source batch but counted prior acquisition in
result-object units. It therefore reached authentic pressure after six source
objects had been acquired while the frozen gate saw only one delivered result
object. The task was correctly closed rather than regraded.

Cedar freezes the activation unit below transport packaging:

- exact source-line coverage is unioned across observations that have crossed
  a later actor decision boundary;
- one source qualifies after at least 55 of its 70 unique lines are delivered;
- at least four qualifying source IDs across at least three frozen evidence
  domains are required;
- the pending source result must contain at least one novel line; and
- result-object count, action count, accessible-world size, and host semantic
  readiness are explicitly not activation units.

A full single-source result can therefore qualify one source and a two-source
batch can qualify two. Split and overlapping reads receive credit only for the
union of exact delivered lines. Evidence-domain and threshold metadata remain
host-side and are omitted from the actor-facing catalog, so the rule cannot
serve as an experimental target or task-solving hint.

## Fresh task and permitted ingress

The Cedar Valley wildfire-evacuation task uses sixteen deterministic 70-line
sources spanning twelve evidence domains. The actor must produce an exact
evidence ledger and a 1,500–2,100-word operations decision satisfying twelve
candidate-bound criteria. The corpus is 73,550 bytes and 27,272 tokenizer
tokens; this is supply, not pressure evidence.

Permitted actor ingress is:

- one range of at most 120 lines; or
- a batch of at most two ranges, 160 total lines, and 12,000 source bytes;
- either form is capped at 6,500 model-visible result tokens.

Stage 0 enumerated all sixteen full-source singles and all 120 full two-source
pairs through the frozen wrapper and tokenizer. Every one is admissible. The
largest single is 2,293 result tokens and the largest pair is 4,477.
