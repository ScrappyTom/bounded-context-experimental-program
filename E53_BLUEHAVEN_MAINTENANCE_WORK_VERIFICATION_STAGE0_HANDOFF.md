## Finite complete-system comparison

- `B1_BATCHED_COUPLED`: every three positive source externalizations trigger
  one bounded maintenance pass that replaces the exact evidence ledger; the
  actor owns decision construction and feedback actions.
- `W1_DIRECT_WORK`: no separate maintenance provider exists; the actor owns
  both exact ledger and decision work.

Both retain exact custody/reopen, deterministic positive first-fit relief,
task-native versioned work, candidate-effect delivery, explicit check

parts of each whole policy. This is not a component main-effect experiment.

different ingress path, construct before pressure, check, submit, or never
pressure. The ordinary screen contains no treatment, relief, maintenance,

one or more protected-pending first-fit substitutions restore feasibility.

thresholds, or call ceiling.
