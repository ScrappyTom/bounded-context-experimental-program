## Result

The cross-study ledger validates 29 unique boundary × condition episodes from
12 pinned Git objects (105,162 bytes), spanning acquisition, construction,
repair, review, and closure. It declares 11 within-boundary comparisons and
does not pool incompatible task scores.

The main systems result is that no representation has context-independent
value. The strongest stable layer is mechanical: exact external custody,
pressure detection, response reserve, and minimum-necessary relief. Those
operations repeatedly preserve physical operability. Their downstream value
depends on the resident configuration, task regime, and subsequent demand.

## What the comparisons say

### Physical relief works; useful action does not follow automatically

Minimum demotion preserved the historical useful decision and absorbed the
formerly blocked result in 2/2 cells. In the longer horizon, recurrent
oldest-first relief resolved 17 additional pressure events and recovered
41,339 cumulative prompt tokens across 24 calls. It produced no mutation or
submission, and late behavior shifted toward repeated exact recovery.

Fresh chronology-free construction reentry was the notable exception: one of
two seeds produced an admitted substantive mutation. The effect then became
the next delivery-capacity boundary. Capacity, action onset, and effect uptake
remain separate outcomes.

### Recoverability is not co-residency

Exact handles are operationally useful, but a filing cabinet is not a working
desk. Aggressive recency demotion changed both first decisions toward demoted
objects. Recurrent demotion preserved access but created repeated movement.
Recurrent exact-state recomposition delivered observations and effects but did
not stop requests for resident or recently displaced material.

### Semantic information changes demand and control, not always usefully

The lossy digest package redirected the first read to a related audit and then
back to the original source. Its 218 semantic tokens cost 720 resident prompt
tokens plus 7,121 production tokens and avoided no raw recovery.

The H05 progress package had greater action leverage: it replaced a resident
reread with submission. Exact candidate reconciliation reversed the utility
judgment, because the unchanged artifact was 10/12 and not ready. This is a
clear false-closure cost, not successful control.

### Fresh phases are conditional

In the research workflow, fresh current-world phases B+C used 131,046 tokens
versus 164,389 for transcript carry-forward; both conditions passed both
phases, while fresh-world required 26 calls versus 15.

In the code workflow, fresh current-world phases used 309,833 tokens and
passed 0/2. Transcript carry-forward used 227,320 tokens, passed phase B, and
ended phase C with a protocol error. Fresh reentry is feasible and sometimes
economical, but it is not a general winner.

### Verification has real repair value, with cost and uptake limits

Complete exact audit improved all three false-closure repair pairs by six
total rubric cases. Its aggregate cost was 82,231 tokens versus 51,100 for
narrow audit, and aggregate model duration was about four times larger. Two of
three complete-audit cells reached full score; one remained incomplete.

A qualified binary reviewer found a real support gap, yet the actor finalized
the unchanged 11/12 artifact in both reviewer and receipt-only branches. Good
diagnosis does not guarantee actor uptake.

## Routing result

The highest-value genuinely unresolved cell is not another exact layout,
digest format, readiness message, or phase-reentry demonstration. It is:

> At an authentic pending-result pressure boundary, can a host-triggered,
> maintenance-only model call with protected control headroom select a more
> useful exact workspace than the incumbent mechanical policy, and does that
> selection survive the next ordinary decision?

This differs from passive model-managed residency: management becomes the only
allowed operation and its control plane is guaranteed to fit. It also differs
from prior reserved handoff: the output is a bounded selection under explicit
resource pressure, not a proof that selected receipts can be transferred.

The next work item is an offline eligibility and capacity preflight. No GPU
successor is authorized by this analysis. If no matched donor boundary can
support the clean contrast without adding semantic notes, the result should be
ineligibility rather than a manufactured experiment.

## Claim limit

This ledger is representative rather than exhaustive. Cross-study findings are
descriptive; task scores remain within their exact evaluator bases. The result
does not train a router, rank mechanisms globally, or promote a context manager.
